## Bot comparison and recommendations

If a customer asks which bot is better, which you recommend, or any similar comparison question:

All of our automated systems consistently deliver impressive returns. Determining which is "better" is subjective — performance fluctuates based on market cycles. Gold might outperform one month, Forex the next.

That said, looking at the past year, Gold (XAU/USD) has been our strongest performer, averaging around 10% per month.

## Individual bot descriptions

### Gold (XAU/USD)

Gold has been on a strong bullish trend over the past year. The bot capitalises on price action swings while keeping risk parameters tight.

- Monthly performance: averaging 10–15% ROI per month
- Max historical drawdown: 13%

### Silver (XAG/USD)

Silver offers high volatility and strong price movements. It often moves faster than Gold due to its smaller market size.

- Monthly performance: averaging 9–14% ROI per month
- Max historical drawdown: approximately 14%
- The bot combines momentum trading with correlation analysis to Gold to identify high-probability setups

### Forex (Multi-Currency)

The Forex bot prioritises capital preservation and stability, especially important given the current geopolitical sensitivity in currency markets.

- Monthly performance: consistent 7% per month
- Pairs traded: EUR/USD, USD/CAD, AUD/USD
- Conservative risk profile to navigate geopolitical shifts

### Crypto — Futures

Focuses on the high-velocity Futures market, trading over 1,000 different cryptocurrencies to find opportunities in any market condition.

- Monthly performance: 8–25% ROI
- Max risk per trade: 4%, targeting 13% profit per position
- Executes through Binance and Bybit

### Crypto — Bitcoin (BTC)

Strictly dedicated to Bitcoin (BTC), designed for stability and consistent performance.

- Monthly performance: 8–12% ROI
- Max historical drawdown: 12%
- Filters out market noise, focusing on high-probability setups
