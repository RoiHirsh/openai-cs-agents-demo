  C8 — Budget minimum ($500) + demo account handling
    Problem: users who don't have $500 should not be led easily to a demo, as demos
    cost the team real setup work with zero revenue (10-day trial only).
    Approach (layered friction by design):
      1. Bot behavior: during budget step, if user is under $500, do NOT mention demo.
         Clarify the minimum and ask if they expect to have $500 available soon, or
         are just exploring. Most will self-select out or confirm they can proceed.
      2. Demo only as last resort: if user explicitly and repeatedly says they cannot
         invest $500 and is about to disengage, the bot may mention demo once — with
         neutral/low-enthusiasm framing (limited 10-day trial, same setup effort as
         a live account).
      3. Confirmation gate (mandatory): before any handoff, bot must ask:
         "Just to confirm — you'd like to move forward with a demo account only,
         with no real investment at this stage?" — this filters out casual interest.
      4. Handoff trigger: handled entirely within bot behavior (onboarding skill),
         NOT via a Supabase handoff scenario. A handoff scenario would only see the
         last message ("yes") with no context. The skill file describes the full
         multi-turn flow, so the agent knows at which exact point to send a closing
         line ("I'll connect you with a team member to get your demo set up") and
         trigger the handoff. No new handoff scenario needed.
      5. FAQ entry: one passive Q&A ("Do you offer a demo account?") that explains
         the demo exists, 10-day trial, requires setup, and is for users not yet
         ready to invest. Answers direct questions without bot proactively offering it.
